WAD File Policy

WAD files contain the game data (levels, textures, sprites, sounds, music) and are separate from the source code. The WAD files are not covered by the GPL and have their own distribution rules.

Shareware WAD (DOOM1.WAD)

The DOOM shareware WAD (DOOM1.WAD) contains Episode 1: "Knee-Deep in the Dead" and may be freely distributed. This is the WAD used by the web player on this site. You can obtain it from numerous sources online, or from the Doom Wiki.

Registered / Commercial WADs

Warning: The full registered DOOM WAD (DOOM.WAD), DOOM II WAD (DOOM2.WAD), and other commercial WADs (TNT.WAD, PLUTONIA.WAD) are commercial products owned by id Software / ZeniMax Media / Microsoft. They may not be freely distributed. You must purchase a legitimate copy to use them.

Commercial WADs can be legally obtained from:

  • Steam
  • GOG.com
  • Physical copies (original floppy disks or CD-ROMs)

This repository does not include any commercial WAD files and never will. Do not submit pull requests containing commercial WAD data.

Free / Community WADs

Community-created WADs and total conversions (such as Freedoom) that are released under open-source or free-content licenses may be used freely with these source ports. Freedoom provides a complete set of IWAD files that are compatible with the DOOM engine and are released under a BSD-style license.
